# Acme Corp Employee Remote Work Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope
Policy Number: POL-HR-014. This policy governs remote work arrangements for all full-time
Acme Corp employees. It applies to permanent employees and long-term contractors, but not to
interns or short-term contractors, who are covered under POL-HR-021.

## 2. Eligibility
An employee is eligible for remote work if they have completed a minimum of six (6) months of
continuous employment, hold a performance rating of "Meets Expectations" or higher on their
most recent review, and have their direct manager's written approval on Form HR-RW-01.

## 3. Approved Work Locations
Remote work must be performed from a location that:
- Is within the employee's country of employment, unless a cross-border exception is granted
  under section 7 of policy POL-HR-014-EXT.
- Has broadband internet with at least 50 Mbps downstream and 10 Mbps upstream.
- Provides a quiet, private area suitable for confidential calls.
Cafes, airports, and other public networks are not approved locations for handling
Confidential or Restricted data.

## 4. Working Hours and Availability
Remote employees must be online and reachable during the core hours of 10:00 to 16:00 in their
local time zone unless an alternate schedule is documented in Form HR-RW-01.
Employees are expected to log a minimum of 40 hours per week for full-time roles.

## 5. Equipment and Reimbursement
Acme Corp provides a company-owned laptop and monitor. Employees may claim up to
USD 500 per calendar year for ergonomic accessories under expense category EXP-ERG-002.
Requests must be submitted within thirty (30) days of purchase, together with a receipt.

## 6. Information Security
All Acme Corp devices used remotely must run the standard Acme-managed OS image, have
full-disk encryption enabled, and connect to internal systems only via the corporate VPN
(configuration profile VPN-CORP-2024). Confidential data must never be stored on personal
devices or personal cloud accounts.

## 7. Termination of Remote Work Privileges
Remote work privileges may be revoked with 14 days notice if the employee fails to meet
availability, performance, or security requirements. Serious violations of section 6 may lead
to immediate revocation and referral to HR under disciplinary policy POL-HR-030.
